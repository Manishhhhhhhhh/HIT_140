HIT140 FIFA WORLD CUP 2026
MY TWO ANALYTIC TASKS - FINAL NUMERICAL RESULTS
============================================================

DATA SOURCE
Official FIFA World Cup 2026 public statistics APIs.

TASK 1 - PASSING PERFORMANCE
------------------------------------------------------------
Question:
Among defenders and midfielders who played at least
90 minutes and attempted at least
20 passes, is there a difference in
average pass-completion rate?

Feature:
pass_completion_rate =
passes_completed / passes_attempted * 100

Sampling:
n = 30 defenders
n = 30 midfielders
Fixed random seed = 42

Defenders:
Mean = 86.36%
95% CI = [83.83%,
          88.90%]

Midfielders:
Mean = 87.87%
95% CI = [85.92%,
          89.82%]

H0: mu_DF = mu_MF
Ha: mu_DF != mu_MF

t = -0.966
df = 29
p = 0.3423

Decision:
Fail to reject H0

Interpretation:
At the 5% significance level, the sample does not provide sufficient statistical evidence that mean pass-completion rate differs between eligible defenders and midfielders.


TASK 2 - SHOOTING ACCURACY
------------------------------------------------------------
Question:
Among forwards and midfielders who played at least
90 minutes and attempted at least
3 shots, is there a difference in
average shot-on-target rate?

Feature:
shot_on_target_rate =
shots_on_target / shots * 100

Sampling:
n = 30 forwards
n = 30 midfielders
Fixed random seed = 142

Forwards:
Mean = 37.34%
95% CI = [28.26%,
          46.43%]

Midfielders:
Mean = 31.77%
95% CI = [24.29%,
          39.26%]

H0: mu_FW = mu_MF
Ha: mu_FW != mu_MF

t = 0.968
df = 29
p = 0.3410

Decision:
Fail to reject H0

Interpretation:
At the 5% significance level, the sample does not provide sufficient statistical evidence that mean shot-on-target rate differs between eligible forwards and midfielders.


LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------
1. This is observational tournament data; statistical differences
   do not demonstrate causation.
2. Eligibility rules are used to avoid highly unstable rates from
   players with extremely little playing time or opportunity.
3. Position classification simplifies complex or hybrid player roles.
4. Sampling introduces sampling error, represented through the
   confidence intervals.
5. Results apply to the defined eligible World Cup 2026 populations,
   not automatically to football players in other competitions.
