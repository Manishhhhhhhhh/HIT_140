TASK 3 — YELLOW CARD DISCIPLINE
===============================

Question:
Among FIFA World Cup 2026 players who played at least 90 minutes,
does average yellow-card rate per 90 minutes differ between defenders
and forwards?

Population:
All eligible FIFA World Cup 2026 defenders and forwards in the master dataset.

Unit of observation:
One player.

Feature:
Yellow Cards per 90 =
Yellow Cards / (Minutes / 90)

Sampling:
- n=30 defenders
- n=30 forwards
- reproducible random sampling

Defenders:
Mean = 0.0659
95% CI = [0.0126, 0.1192]

Forwards:
Mean = 0.0969
95% CI = [0.0022, 0.1916]

H0: mu_DF = mu_FW
Ha: mu_DF != mu_FW

t = -0.5832
p = 0.562597

Decision:
Fail to reject H0

Conclusion:
There is insufficient statistical evidence at the 5% level to conclude that mean yellow-card rate per 90 minutes differs between eligible defenders and forwards.

Limitation:
The analysis uses observational tournament data. Position does not necessarily
cause disciplinary differences, and playing style, match context, refereeing,
and team tactics may also influence yellow-card rates.
